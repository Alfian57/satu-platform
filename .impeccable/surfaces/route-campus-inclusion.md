# Campus Inclusion Review

## Job and Audience

Authorized campus staff meninjau pola partisipasi yang mungkin membutuhkan kesempatan tambahan atau pemeriksaan data. Ini adalah restricted operational surface. Mode: **Operate**.

## Outcome and Proof

Reviewer memahami data sufficiency, faktor operasional, score version, dan pilihan tindakan aman. Setiap outcome berasal dari human review.

## Selected Direction

Mewarisi **Buku Besar Kolaborasi** sebagai restricted exception register. Visual lebih tenang daripada alert dashboard: evidence, uncertainty, dan reason mendapat ruang lebih besar daripada severity color.

## Scope and Boundaries

Mencakup restricted queue, explanation, recent collaboration context, acknowledge, dismiss, outreach note, dan audit. Tidak mencakup diagnosis, recruiter exposure, public label, automated counseling referral, atau punitive action.

## States and Ranges

- Feature disabled dan synthetic-demo-only.
- Insufficient data: tidak membuat signal.
- New, acknowledged, dismissed, outreach recorded, expired.
- Data artifact, recently joined, no available project, repeated low opportunity.
- 0–100 queue items; large history paginated.

## Interaction and Layout

Reviewer melihat “why this appeared” dan uncertainty sebelum action. Outreach memakai ordinary opportunity/support language. Dismiss dan outreach membutuhkan reason. Surface tidak menggunakan red severity sebagai default.

## Constraints and Open Decisions

DPIA, lawful basis, minimum sample, retention, reviewer role, dan support policy harus selesai sebelum data nyata. Laravel Pennant memisahkan disabled, synthetic, dan approved real activation. Recruiter dan student shared props tidak pernah memuat signal.
